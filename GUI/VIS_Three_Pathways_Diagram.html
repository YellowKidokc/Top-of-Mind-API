THE MASTER EQUATION OF RECOVERY
dC/dt = O · G(1−C) − S · C
Path 1: G=0, O=0 → dC/dt = −S·C → Exponential decay
Path 2: G=0, O=high → dC/dt ≈ −S·C → Slow decay under strain
Path 3: G>0, O>0 → dC/dt = O·G(1−C) − S·C → Recovery possible
WHY "PRAISE EVEN THOUGH YOU DON'T FEEL IT" WORKS
The brain responds to the behavioral signal regardless of the emotional state. When you speak thanksgiving out loud, the vagus nerve activates. The parasympathetic cascade begins. Oxytocin releases. Serotonin increases. The feeling follows the action, not the other way around.

You're not pretending. You're training the neural pathway before the emotion catches up. The behavior rewires the circuit. This is neuroplasticity operating in real time.

The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom — because genuine acknowledgment that you are not the highest authority in your own life is what fires the vmPFC, activates relational circuitry, drops cortisol, and opens the closed system. It is the neural precondition for receiving grace.
